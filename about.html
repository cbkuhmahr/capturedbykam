About

Behind
the frame.

Captured by Kam is the visual work of Kamar Mosley—a husband, father, and Midwest-based storyteller focused on images that feel honest, clean, and lasting.

The camera is there to pay attention—not get in the way.

Before earning my business degree, I studied journalism at Eastern Michigan University, writing features and providing photos for The Eastern Echo.

That background still shapes how I work: notice the details, understand the people, and tell the story without forcing it.

Approach

Genuine emotion. Clean lines.

From youth sports and senior portraits to headshots, events, and cinematic video, every project is approached with curiosity first and polish second.
