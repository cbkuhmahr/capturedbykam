ABOUT / THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Kamar
Mosley.

I'm the photographer and videographer behind Captured by Kam, an independent visual studio based in Toledo, Ohio. I work across sports, portraiture, events, and creative projects—bringing an editorial perspective to every assignment.

Pay attention to the moment. Make it worth keeping.

My foundation is in journalism. I studied at Eastern Michigan University, writing features and contributing photography to The Eastern Echo before earning my business degree.

That experience taught me to observe before directing. I look for honest expressions, clear composition, and the details that give a story its identity. The goal is not simply to make a good-looking image. It's to make the right one.
